My Happy Place

Thrumming beats fill my veins,

Smooth riding down the highway,

Wind in my hair flowing without care,

My heart drumming to the base.

Every moment gives me peace

The only time I truly unwind

Away from the storm of chaos in my mind.

Breathing air that has no binds,

Free from cages of insanity

No chains to tether me.

A momentary break from the brain,

No thoughts that berate.

Parades of tenses

Fruity scents invade my senses

Leave you breathless,

Faceless mentions.

Tapping my fingers with ease,

Music enlightens and frees,

Facing the between

That I cannot see separately

Entangled as one being

The happy place is the only place to be.

Metamorphosis

Strings of symphonies greet me

Flutters of change existing

Allow the wings of a butterfly to morph

Lengths and colors unknown

A third metamorphosis that doesn't exist

Right in front of you

Staring through eyes filled on life of mystery

Not yet spoken as you haven't turned the page

Calling to you from beyond the stage

Shutter and curl

Cocoon this world

Shake and crumble ~ Layers awaste

Tempting all fate

Rays shimmer over the kaleidoscope

When finally put to rest

No reflection left

To bear any resemblance

Life begins again

Time will remember

The colors.

Comfort

It starts with soft

Bundled in cotton, not cloth

Holding close a feather plush

Perfect hug

Stays embraced

Ambience surrounds

Drips, Taps

Running water streams its way on glass

Sound so loved in my ears

Purrs and whispers stay near

Vibrating waves up each arm

Curled up on my lap

Reaching for chocolatey delights

Warm steam wisp in the air

Velvety wear, sleepy slippers, silky hair

Light embers crackling in the chimney

This comfort completes me.

Autophobia

Creaks on the floorboards

Steps through the quiet

Voices ring in my head

Whispers in midst the silence

The familiar tingle of uncomfort lingers

Creeping anxiety

It holds no propriety

Logic found senseless

Heightened senses

Wishing I couldn't hear

Hoping it's just fear

Rather ignorance instead of brilliance

No proof worth this

I want to ignore but every noise near my door

Any clink or tap sends chills down my back

This old creaky house

That has a parade made with every wind its way

Will always send me clinging for my hollow bat

Dear Betsy, you protect me

But can you save me from my mind?

As my spine starts to wind

And my thoughts start to tumble

In worry and fright

That I just might

Not be the only one in this old house tonight.
